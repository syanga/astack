The ladder ranks the evidence for one claim, weakest first.

1. **You said so.** No evidence.
2. **You pointed at the line.** A real `file:line`, or the library's own source at its pinned version.
3. **You walked the path.** Step by step, you showed whether the case reaches.
4. **You ran it.** A script or test that calls the real code and fails when you are wrong. One small script that imports the library the app ships with and calls the exact function is the usual form.
5. **You observed it in the running app.**

A finding that cannot reach rung 2 is unverified.
